Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the security of your library data are important to us.

Information We Collect

LibControl may process information required to provide library-management services, including library details, student details, login information, attendance records, seat information and notices.

Authentication & Security

User authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. Passwords are not stored directly in the LibControl Firestore database. Access to library information is designed around authenticated users and library-specific authorization.

Use of Information

Information is used to provide and operate the LibControl library-management platform, maintain account access, manage library operations and improve the reliability of the service.

Data Protection

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. Access to information is intended to be limited according to the user's role and library authorization.

Third-Party Services

LibControl uses third-party infrastructure and services, including Firebase, for authentication and data-related functionality. Their respective policies may also apply to information processed through those services.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy, data handling or your library account, please contact the LibControl team.
